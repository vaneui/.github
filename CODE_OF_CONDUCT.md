# Code of conduct

VaneUI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in every repository of the organization: issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.

In short:

- Be respectful, and assume the other person means well.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No publishing of someone else's private information.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks these rules to **hello@vaneui.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in handling it.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break the code of conduct, and may block a person from the organization for a time or for good.
